# Keyword map (Google Keyword Planner, October 2026)

Source: Google Keyword Planner, last 12 months, 10 seed groups covering every service, run separately for the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, India and UAE. 6,936 keywords collected; 6,193 relevant after filtering. Full data: `keywords.tsv`; scoring and page mapping: `analyze.py`.

Volumes are Google's monthly ranges (exact numbers need an active ad campaign). Score = volume weighted by market priority (US 1.0, UK 0.8, AU/CA 0.7, IN 0.5, UAE 0.3) and intent (hiring/service searches ×3, how-to/problem ×1.5, tool searches ×0.35).

Out of scope and deliberately not targeted: Shopify theme/web development and design, PPC/advertising, link-building SEO, Klaviyo agency work. These have high volume but bring leads for services we don't sell.
