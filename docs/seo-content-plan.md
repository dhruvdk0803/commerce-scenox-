# SEO / AEO content plan

Research date: October 2026. Based on live SERP sampling (US-biased search tool, country words added for IN/UK/AU) and competitor site reviews. No search volumes were available: verify priorities in Google Search Console and a keyword tool before scaling.

## What the research found

- **Nobody publishes prices.** Competitors (SunTec India, Team4eCom, Invensis, Damco, Rely Services, Dinarys) are quote-only. Only Fiverr/Upwork gigs and one small site show numbers. Our public per-market pricing is the strongest differentiator in every SERP.
- **US, UK and AU searchers get Indian BPO pages** with no local currency, tax or spelling. No local specialist ranks.
- **Weak SERPs (fastest wins):** WooCommerce product upload, Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho listing (India), "catalog management service" (cluttered with PIM software lists), done-for-you WhatsApp / AI support / abandoned cart (SaaS-dominated), AI product imagery as a managed service, feed-only fixed-price management.
- **"Best X agency" queries** are served by directories (Sortlist, DesignRush) and vendor-written lists. No specialist list exists for catalog/listing agencies.
- **Competitor blogs are thin and off-topic** (Amazon PPC, accounting). Almost nobody has deep Shopify CSV / migration / Merchant Center content with worked examples.
- **Winning architecture pattern:** one landing page per service and platform, plus case studies with numbers.

## Rules (why we don't copy or spin)

Rewriting competitor pages is copyright infringement and triggers Google's scaled content abuse policy, which demotes the whole domain. Every page must add something competitors don't have: our prices, worked examples, real error fixes, templates, market-specific detail. Brief: `docs/guide-writing.md`.

## Wave 2: commercial landing pages (highest lead impact)

1. Platform service pages: `/shopify-product-upload`, `/woocommerce-product-upload`, `/magento-product-upload`, `/bigcommerce-product-upload`, `/wix-product-upload`
2. Marketplace listing (India): `/amazon-flipkart-listing`
3. Country pages: product upload service India / USA / UK / Australia (local prices, tax, platforms, spelling)
4. Migration pairs: Magento, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace → Shopify
5. "Service, not software": ecommerce catalog management services
6. Comparison: Scenox vs freelancers vs in-house vs apps
7. Industry pages: fashion, jewellery, electronics, beauty, furniture catalog upload
8. Best ecommerce listing agencies (transparent method, discloses we are one of the options)

## Wave 3: programmatic but genuinely useful guides

- Shopify / WooCommerce / Magento CSV error pages (one per real error, with broken and fixed rows): ~35-45
- Merchant Center disapproval reasons (one per reason): ~20
- Product description templates per niche: ~10-15
- Image requirements per channel: ~8
- Shopify tax/payments setup per country: ~12
Publish in batches of 10-20, check Search Console, noindex or merge anything that doesn't earn impressions within ~3 months.
