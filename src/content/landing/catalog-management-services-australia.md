Australian stores deal with distance, and it shows up in the data. Suppliers ship from overseas with copy written for another market. Weights are missing, which turns freight into guesswork. Prices come in USD, then get converted by hand. This page covers how we fix catalogue data for an Australian store, and what is specific to selling here.

## What is specific to Australian catalogues

### GST is in the price

Australian shoppers see GST-inclusive prices, and the Australian Taxation Office expects the price shown to include it. If your supplier sends ex-GST cost prices and your store adds 10%, the maths needs to be right in every system. We map where each price lives. For overseas sellers, the situation is different: in many cases a marketplace operator collects GST on low-value goods. Whether that applies to you is a question for your accountant.

### ABN, GST status and seller identity

Marketplaces and tax invoices lean on seller identity. We do not hold or change your ABN details, but we do keep product-level fields that depend on them, such as tax status flags, consistent so that the right products are marked as taxable.

### Distance and freight

Australia Post, Sendle, Aramex and courier rate tables depend on weight and cubic dimensions, and shipping to WA, NT or regional Queensland can cost several times a Sydney delivery. A missing weight means a wrong quote, or a free-shipping promise that costs you. We chase down missing and implausible weights, such as a 20 kg cushion, before you publish.

### Sizing

Australian clothing sizing follows its own scale, closer to UK than to US for womenswear, and swimwear and kids' ranges use numeric sizes by age. We keep AU sizes as the primary option and put conversions into a size chart. We do not assume a UK size maps to an AU one without checking your brand's own chart.

### Marketplaces and buy-now-pay-later

Kogan, eBay AU and Amazon.com.au have their own category trees and required fields. Many Australian stores also show Afterpay or Zip messaging that depends on price fields being accurate, so a bad price breaks the message. We hold one master record and produce channel versions, so a change is made once.

### Seasons

Australian seasons run opposite to the northern hemisphere. Suppliers in the US and Europe tag items "Summer 2026" and ship them in Australia's winter. We retag seasonal products so collections and feeds follow your calendar.
