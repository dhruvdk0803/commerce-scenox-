Product data entry is the dullest job in ecommerce and one of the costliest when it goes wrong. A wrong size on a variant becomes a return. A missing material field becomes a rejected feed item. A price typed with a misplaced decimal becomes a very bad afternoon.

Scenox Commerce handles product data entry as a managed service. You send source material, and you receive structured, store-ready data.

## What you get

- Source data extracted into your platform's exact import format, whether Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix or a custom build
- Titles, SKUs, prices, weights, dimensions, barcodes and stock levels mapped to the right columns
- Variants and options built from the supplier's size and colour logic
- Attribute values standardised: units, capitalisation, colour names, materials
- Images matched to the right product and variant
- A QA report of fills, flags and unresolved items

## How it works

1. You send spreadsheets, PDFs, images or a store export, and tell us the target platform.
2. We inspect the files and send a fixed quote and a written delivery date.
3. The AI engine extracts and structures the data; a person reviews every output against the source documents.
4. You approve in a draft or staging state, with a list of any flagged items for your decision.
5. We load the data through a staff account, or hand over a ready-to-import file.

## Why AI plus review beats manual double-keying

The old defence against typing errors was double-keying: two people enter the same data and a system flags the differences. It works in principle. In practice it doubles the labour, it is slow, and both operators can make the same mistake when the source is ambiguous, such as a smudged PDF or a supplier abbreviation.

Our process changes where the human effort goes.

**Extraction is not typing.** The AI engine reads the source and produces structured fields directly. Nobody retypes a 14-character SKU, so nobody transposes two characters in it.

**Rules run on every row.** Before a person sees anything, the data is checked against rules: required fields present, prices numeric, variants sharing consistent option sets, units standardised, duplicates flagged.

**The reviewer checks, rather than creates.** A person compares output against the source and focuses on the cases the engine marked as uncertain. Reviewing is faster than keying and catches a different class of error: values that are formatted correctly but wrong.

**Ambiguity goes to you.** If a PDF says "approx. 30cm" or two suppliers give different weights, we do not guess silently. It appears in the QA report as a question for you.

We do not publish accuracy percentages, because they depend entirely on how clean your sources are. The QA report gives you the real picture for your own data, product by product.

## Inputs we deal with every week

| Source | Typical trouble | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier Excel | Merged cells, one product per several rows, notes in the SKU column | Normalise structure, split notes into their own fields |
| PDF line sheet | Tables that copy badly, images embedded with text | Extract by region, verify against the page |
| Image-only catalog | Specs in photos | Read, structure, flag low-confidence values |
| Old store export | Duplicate SKUs, orphan variants | Rebuild parent and child relationships |
| Manufacturer feed | Different taxonomy from yours | Map to your categories and attributes |
