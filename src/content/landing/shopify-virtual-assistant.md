A Shopify virtual assistant is usually hired for the same chores: adding the new season's products, updating prices from a supplier file, fixing stock counts, putting products into collections and tidying images. It is steady work that expands the moment you get busy.

We provide that as a team service. You do not manage a person, and every output is reviewed before it goes live. We are honest about scope: this is catalog work, not general admin.

## What you get

- New products added from your spreadsheets, supplier files or PDFs, with titles, descriptions, variants and prices
- Price and stock updates from a sheet, matched by SKU, with a before and after file
- Collections created and maintained, manual or automated by rules
- Images uploaded, matched to products and variants, and named sensibly
- SEO fields filled: page titles, meta descriptions, handles and alt text
- Metafields populated for specs where your theme uses them
- Draft and archived status changes, tags and product type clean-up
- A short monthly QA note listing what changed and what we could not resolve

## What we do not do

We do not run ads, answer customer messages, process or fulfil orders, post on social media or build theme features. We do not take your store passwords. Tasks outside the catalog are declined, not improvised.

## How it works

1. You send a first batch and describe your store and routine tasks.
2. We set the plan, confirm what counts as routine and send a written quote.
3. Each task: you send a file or brief, the AI engine does the volume, a person reviews.
4. You approve changes via a before and after file or a draft state.
5. We apply them through a staff account, or return an import file.

## Shopify constraints that shape the work

| Quirk | Why it matters | How we handle it |
|---|---|---|
| Three options per product | Size, colour and a third option are the limit on one product | Plan variants before import, flag products needing more |
| Variants limit | Large size runs can hit the per-product variant cap | Check counts first, split with a clear rule if needed |
| Handles | Changing one changes the URL | Keep existing, redirect if changed |
| Inventory by location | Stock updates need the right location | Confirm locations before updating stock |
| Smart collections | Rules depend on tags, vendor, type or price | Standardise those fields first |
| Metafields | Specs need defined fields | Create definitions, fill in bulk |

For the import side, see [Shopify bulk product upload](/guides/shopify-bulk-product-upload), and for collections see [Shopify collections](/guides/shopify-collections).

## Hiring a VA or using a managed plan

A hired assistant gives you someone available for odd jobs and judgement calls, but you carry the training, checking and cover for absence. A managed plan gives you a predictable monthly cost, review built in and a team behind each task, with narrower scope. Many stores use both: a person for daily admin, us for catalog volume. The trade-offs are covered in [outsourcing product upload](/guides/outsource-product-upload).

## Is this a fit?

It suits stores that add or change products every week or month, work with supplier files and have no one on staff to spare for it. Agencies with several small clients also use it.

It does not suit you if you want someone for customer service, ads or general admin, or if you change a few products a month and can do it yourself in minutes.
