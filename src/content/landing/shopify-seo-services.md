We do that work at volume, with a person checking it. We do not do link building, PPC or theme development, and nothing here comes with a ranking promise.

## Shopify specifics that matter

### SEO fields and length

Each product, collection, page and blog post has a "Search engine listing" section with a page title and meta description. Left blank, Shopify falls back to the product title and the start of the description. Search engines truncate long titles and descriptions, so we write to roughly 60 and 155 characters rather than trust the fallback.

### Handles and redirects

The handle is the URL slug. Editing it on a live product changes the address, which is how rankings get lost. In the product editor, Shopify offers to create a redirect when you change a handle. In bulk, URL redirects go in through Online Store, Navigation, URL redirects, or a CSV import of "Redirect from" and "Redirect to" columns. We always deliver that file. See the [301 redirect mapping guide](/guides/migration-301-redirects).

### The /collections/x/products/y path

Shopify serves a product at /products/handle and also at /collections/collection-handle/products/handle. Shopify's SEO overview says it adds canonical tags automatically to reduce duplicate content, and standard themes point the canonical at the plain product URL. We do not rewrite your theme. We check the canonical in your page source during the audit, and if collection-scoped URLs are being indexed or linked internally, we tell you and note what a developer should change.

### Metafields for specs

Material, dimensions, care and compatibility belong in structured places, not buried in a paragraph. Metafields let your theme show them in a spec table and keep them consistent. We fill them in bulk; see [importing metafields in bulk](/guides/shopify-metafields-import).

### Alt text

Alt text is set per image on the product. Good alt text describes what is in the image: "Navy wool crew-neck jumper, front view." We write them from your product data and variant names.

### Tags versus collections

Tags are a filter label. A collection is a page with its own URL, title, meta description and copy, so it can rank. When a tag represents something people search for ("linen shirts"), it is usually better as a collection. See [Shopify collections, manual and automated](/guides/shopify-collections).
