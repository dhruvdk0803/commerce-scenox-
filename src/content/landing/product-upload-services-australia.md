## What is different about selling in Australia

### GST-inclusive display

Australian consumers expect a shelf price that includes the 10% GST. Supplier lists often quote ex-GST wholesale, which creates the classic error: half the sheet is ex-GST and half is inclusive. We reconcile that against your instruction before upload, so a supplier cost does not become a selling price by accident. Platform settings are covered in our [Shopify GST Australia guide](/guides/shopify-gst-australia).

### Marketplaces

| Channel | What we prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon AU | Flat file, browse node, variation theme, bullets, backend terms. See [Amazon listing](/amazon-product-listing-service). |
| eBay AU | Item specifics, condition, category, variations, shipping profile fields. |
| Kogan | Product data and identifiers; approval depends on category and seller status. |

Each marketplace has its own seller approval. We cannot get you accepted; we make sure the data is not the reason you are rejected.

### ACL-friendly product information

Australian Consumer Law expects claims to be accurate and not misleading. For catalogue work, that means we write from your specs, not from imagination. If a supplier says "waterproof" and the spec sheet says "water-resistant", we use the sheet and flag the difference. Warranty wording stays as you give it. We are not a legal service, so anything with legal weight is yours to approve.

### Distance and shipping data

Large parcels cross large distances, so accurate weights and cubic dimensions matter more here than almost anywhere. A missing weight on a bulky item can turn a shipping rate into a loss.
