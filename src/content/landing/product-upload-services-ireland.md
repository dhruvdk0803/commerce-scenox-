## What is different about selling in Ireland

### VAT, with several rates

Irish VAT is not one number. The standard rate is 23%, with reduced rates for categories such as some food, fuel and printed material, and a zero rate for others. A catalogue spanning several categories can legitimately carry several rates. We keep a tax-class column in the build so each product is flagged consistently, but you or your accountant decide the class. Consumer prices are normally VAT-inclusive, so we convert and check each row on the basis you confirm. Rates change, so check Revenue for the current schedule.

### EU product information

Since the General Product Safety Regulation applied in December 2024, online listings for consumer goods are expected to carry identification of the product and details of the manufacturer or an EU-based responsible person, along with safety information and warnings. Requirements depend on the product type. Our role is practical: we give those items a home on the product page, copy them from your supplier documents, and list any product with a missing field in the QA report. We do not verify claims.
