UK catalogue work looks simple until you list the same product on four channels. Prices must show VAT, sizing must be UK, and a marketplace wants a barcode your supplier never gave you. This page covers how we handle those details.

## What you get

- Products created on Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce or Magento from your files.
- Titles and descriptions in British English, with metric measurements.
- Variants, options and SKUs built correctly.
- Prices uploaded as VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive, consistently, as you instruct.
- EAN, GTIN and MPN fields checked for format and duplicates.
- Weights in grams or kilograms for Royal Mail and courier rates.
- Material and care text kept as supplied, with compliance wording passed through to the listing where you provide it.
- Images matched with alt text, plus a QA report.

## How it works

1. **Send your files.** CSV, Excel, supplier price lists, PDFs or an export from another platform.
2. **Fixed quote.** We count products and variants and send a price in pounds before starting.
3. **We build.** AI does the volume and a person reviews every output.
4. **You approve.** Products arrive as drafts with a QA report.
5. **Publish.** We work through a staff or collaborator account you can remove, never a shared password.

## What is different about selling in the UK

### VAT-inclusive pricing

A UK shopper expects the price on the page to be the price they pay. We handle the field details. For example, a supplier sheet showing £10.00 ex-VAT becomes £12.00 at the standard 20% rate, but only if you confirm the product sits at that rate. Some items are reduced-rated or zero-rated, so we do not guess. Mixed inclusive and exclusive rows in one file are the most common error we correct. For platform settings see our [Shopify VAT guide](/guides/shopify-vat-uk).

### Marketplaces

| Channel | What we prepare |
| --- | --- |
| eBay UK | Item specifics, condition, category, variations, postage profile fields. |
| Amazon UK | Flat file, browse node, variation theme, bullets, backend terms. See [Amazon listing](/amazon-product-listing-service). |
| OnBuy | Catalogue data, identifiers, category attributes, images. |

Marketplaces lean heavily on barcodes. If your own-brand products have none, we explain the GS1 UK route rather than invent codes.

### UKCA and CE marking text

Some categories, such as toys, electricals and certain protective gear, need conformity marking and traceable manufacturer or importer information. We copy what you supply into the right place on the product page and into marketplace compliance fields. We do not verify the claim. If a product has no supplied evidence, we mark it in the QA report instead of leaving a gap.

### GDPR and data handling

Product files rarely hold personal data, but customer lists and order exports sometimes arrive mixed in. We ask you to strip them out, work only through accounts you can revoke, and do not use your data to train third-party models.
