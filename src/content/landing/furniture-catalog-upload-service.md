## What furniture catalogs need

### Dimensions that mean something

A sofa listing needs overall width, depth and height, plus seat height and depth, and often door or stair clearance. Suppliers mix centimetres and inches, and sometimes swap width and depth. We pick one display unit for your market, store the numbers consistently, and flag any figure that looks implausible, such as a dining chair 9 cm tall.

### Weight and packaging

Product dimensions and shipping dimensions are different. A flat-pack wardrobe may be 200 cm tall but ship in two cartons of different sizes. We keep both. Shipping profiles then price by carton, and a store can separate items that go by parcel from those that need pallet or freight delivery.

### Freight and delivery

Large items often need a different shipping profile: delivery to kerb or room of choice, lead times, and regions that cannot be reached. We set up the product-level fields (weight, carton count, lead time, freight class where applicable) so rates can be configured in your platform or shipping app. We do not negotiate carrier rates.

### Finishes and fabrics

A sofa in 40 fabrics and 3 leg finishes is 120 combinations. Shopify's three-option limit and variant cap make that a poor fit, so we usually treat fabric as the main option and leg finish as an add-on or separate product. WooCommerce and Magento can handle more, but a very long variant list slows the page. We agree an approach before building.

### Assembly

We record whether the piece arrives assembled, needs assembly, the typical number of people and tools needed, and the instruction file where available.

### Imagery

Furniture sells on context. We order images as: hero on white, lifestyle, detail, dimension drawing. Each finish needs its own hero. Missing finishes are listed in the QA report.

## Returns, delivery and room context

Furniture returns are expensive, so the page needs to prevent a wrong purchase. We structure product dimensions, seat height, clearance, weight capacity and the packaged dimensions in one consistent format. We also add delivery notes you supply: lead time, white-glove or kerbside options, assembly time and whether tools are included.

Material and finish detail should be searchable: wood species, fabric type, cleaning code and colour finish names that match the swatch image. We also link related pieces, such as matching sets, tables with chairs, or modular sections, so shoppers can see a full range. Where you sell by room, we build collections for living, dining, bedroom and outdoor.
