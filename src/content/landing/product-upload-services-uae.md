## What is different about selling in the UAE

### Arabic and right-to-left

Arabic content is not just translation. Product names mix Arabic with Latin brand names and model numbers, and numerals, units and punctuation can flip order when pasted into a right-to-left field. We check how mixed strings render, keep model numbers and SKUs in a safe form, and avoid pushing Arabic into fields that your theme or platform does not display RTL. Your theme must support RTL layout; we flag it if a draft shows broken alignment, but theme work is a separate job.

### Dirhams, VAT and display

UAE shoppers expect to see AED. A store can display AED through its currency or market settings while the catalogue stores one base price. The UAE standard VAT rate is 5%, and shoppers normally expect to see the final price. We make sure every row in a supplier file is on one basis before upload; a sheet that mixes the two is the most common error we fix. We do not decide whether a product is zero-rated or exempt.

COD is common, so catalogues benefit from tidy variant names and weights that match the real parcel. See our [COD confirmation guide](/guides/whatsapp-cod-confirmation) for the order-side flow. If you ship to Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman, rules differ by country; we add per-market fields such as harmonised codes only where you supply them.
