New Zealand is a small market with big-market expectations: shoppers compare you with Australian and US sites, TradeMe still matters, and freight makes weights and dimensions unusually important. This page explains what we do about that.

## What you get

- Products created on Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce or Magento from your files.
- Titles and descriptions in New Zealand English, with metric measurements.
- Variants, options and SKUs built correctly.
- Prices uploaded GST-inclusive or exclusive, consistently, as you instruct.
- Weights and dimensions checked for courier tables, since rural delivery costs vary.
- Barcodes checked for format and duplicates.
- Images matched with alt text, plus a QA report.

## How it works

1. **Send your files.** Spreadsheets, supplier lists, PDFs or an export from an old store.
2. **Fixed quote.** We count products and variants and give you one price.
3. **We build.** The AI engine does the volume and a person reviews every output.
4. **You approve.** Products arrive as drafts for review.
5. **Publish.** We work through a staff or collaborator account you can remove, never a shared password.

## What is different about selling in New Zealand

### GST-inclusive pricing

NZ consumers expect the shelf price to be the final price. The standard GST rate is 15%. A supplier sheet showing NZ$100.00 ex-GST becomes NZ$115.00, but only if you confirm the basis. Wholesale sheets often mix the two, and the discrepancy tends to appear only after publishing. We convert and check before the draft is built, and we leave rate decisions to you.

### Marketplaces

| Channel | What we prepare |
| --- | --- |
| TradeMe | Category and attribute fields, titles, photos, shipping and pricing fields. |
| Amazon | Flat file, browse node, variation theme, bullets, backend terms. See [Amazon listing](/amazon-product-listing-service). |
| Shopify, WooCommerce | Full store build with NZD display. |

TradeMe buyers search differently from Amazon buyers: shorter titles, condition and location matter, and offers or reserve prices are common. We write marketplace titles separately from store titles instead of reusing them.

### Selling across the Tasman

Many Kiwi stores sell into Australia too. If you plan to, tell us at the start so we can build separate AUD price fields and Australian copy. The [Australian GST guide](/guides/shopify-gst-australia) covers the other side. Check import rules with NZ Customs.
