Most ecommerce SEO advice is about blogs, backlinks and site speed. Meanwhile the catalog itself, which is where products actually rank and sell, is full of titles like "Item 4471 - Blue", empty meta descriptions and 600 products sharing the same supplier paragraph.

That is the layer we work on. This page is deliberately narrow about what we do and what we do not.

## What you get

- Product title rewrites that put the product type, brand and key attribute first, within sensible length
- Meta titles and meta descriptions written per product and collection, unique across the store
- Product descriptions rewritten where thin or duplicated, built only from facts in your source data
- Clean handles and URLs, with a 301 redirect file for anything we change
- Image alt text and image file names that describe the product
- The data fields structured data reads from: brand, GTIN, MPN, material, colour, size, condition
- Duplicate and thin content cleanup across products and variants
- Internal linking through collection structure and collection descriptions
- Alignment with your Google Merchant Center feed, so titles and attributes match on both sides
- A before and after file and a QA report listing every change and every item we could not resolve

## What we do not do

We do not build links, buy placements or run outreach. We do not manage PPC or shopping ads. We do not develop or edit themes, and we do not write ongoing blog content. We do not promise rankings. If your problem is a slow theme or no backlinks, a catalog cleanup will not fix it, and we will say so in the audit.

## How it works

1. You send a catalog export and the platform name (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix or custom).
2. We audit the catalog and report what is missing, duplicated or malformed, then send a fixed quote and delivery date.
3. The AI engine drafts the changes at volume; a person reviews every output against your source data.
4. You approve a before and after file, and a draft or staging state where the platform allows it.
5. We apply the changes through a staff or collaborator account, or hand you an import file. Redirects are delivered with it.

## Catalog SEO problems we see most

| Problem | Why it hurts | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier text pasted on many stores | Near-identical pages compete and add little | Rewrite from your product facts |
| Titles led by SKU or colour | Weak match to how people search | Reorder to type, brand, key attribute |
| Empty or auto-generated meta descriptions | Search shows a random snippet | Write one per page |
| Image files named IMG_2231.jpg, empty alt text | Lost image search and accessibility | Descriptive names and alt text |
| Variants as separate near-duplicate products | Splits relevance across pages | Advise consolidation; build parent and child where needed |
| Missing GTIN, brand or material | Merchant Center items limited or rejected | Fill from source, flag gaps |
| Collections with no copy | Thin category pages | Short, specific collection descriptions and linked subcollections |
