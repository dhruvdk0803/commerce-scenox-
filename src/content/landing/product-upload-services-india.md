Most Indian sellers send us a messy Excel sheet, a folder of supplier PDFs, and photos named after a WhatsApp group. That is fine. This page explains what we do with those files, how the India-specific fields are handled, and what it costs in rupees.

## What you get

- Products created on Shopify (India store) or WooCommerce from your sheets, PDFs or an existing marketplace export.
- Titles, descriptions and tags written to a consistent pattern, in Indian English spelling (colour, jewellery, kurta).
- Variants built correctly: size, colour, fabric or pack size, with SKUs and prices on every row.
- MRP and selling price set the way you want them shown, including compare-at price for discounts.
- HSN code and GST rate placed in the field your store or invoicing app reads, ready for your review.
- Weight and dimensions on every product so shipping rates and COD rules calculate properly.
- Images matched to products and variants, with alt text.
- A QA report listing anything we could not resolve, such as a missing size or an unreadable price.

## How it works

1. **Send your files.** Excel, CSV, supplier PDFs, a Google Sheet or an export from another store. Photos can be a shared Drive or Dropbox folder.
2. **Fixed quote.** We inspect the files, count products and variants, and send a rupee price before any work starts.
3. **We build.** Our AI engine does the volume work and a person reviews every output, including prices and tax fields.
4. **You approve.** Everything sits as draft products first. You check a sample and the QA report.
5. **Publish.** After your approval we publish. We work through a staff or collaborator account you can remove at any time. We never ask for your store password.

## What is different about selling in India

Indian catalogues carry details that a generic upload service skips. These are the ones we handle.

| Detail | How we treat it |
| --- | --- |
| GST | Store tax settings do the calculation. We keep prices consistently GST-inclusive or exclusive, never mixed across rows. |
| HSN | One code per product type, placed in a metafield or the invoicing app's field. Unclear items are flagged for your CA. |
| MRP | Printed MRP and selling price kept as separate numbers, so MRP display is not lost. |
| COD | Weight, dimensions and a COD-eligible tag, so high-risk items can be excluded in your rules. |
| Shipping | Shipping profiles kept clean so courier rate checks use real weights. |
| Names | Brand and fabric names standardised: "cotton blend", not five spellings of it. |

### Shopify India and WooCommerce

On Shopify we work inside the three-options-per-product limit and use metafields for fabric, care, country of origin and HSN where your theme can display them. On WooCommerce we build attributes and variations properly, so filters work, and keep GST plugin fields populated. For platform settings, see our [Shopify GST guide](/guides/shopify-gst-india).

### Cross-listing to Amazon.in, Flipkart, Meesho and Myntra

A clean store catalogue is the best starting point for marketplace listings, because the same titles, attributes and images get reused. Each marketplace has its own category templates and mandatory fields, so we treat that as a separate feeds job. See [Flipkart and Meesho listing](/flipkart-meesho-listing-service) and [Amazon listing](/amazon-product-listing-service). Myntra and brand-led panels often need onboarding by the marketplace first; we prepare your data, but account approval is theirs to give.

## Is this a fit?

It suits you if you have a few hundred to a few thousand SKUs, supplier data in several formats, and no time to type. It also suits sellers moving from marketplace-only selling to their own store.

If you have under 30 products and a clean sheet, upload them yourself; Shopify's CSV import is fine for that. If your real problem is tax filing, invoice formats or courier negotiation, that is outside our scope and your CA or logistics partner should handle it.
