Amazon does not forgive sloppy data. A wrong category, a missing variation attribute or a poor main image can mean a suppressed listing, and fixing a suppressed listing one row at a time is slow. We prepare the catalogue properly before it is submitted.

## What you get

- A category-correct flat file (or bulk upload sheet) per product type.
- Browse node selection for each product, checked against Amazon's category tree for that market.
- Parent and child variations with the right theme and attributes.
- Titles written to category rules, with brand, product type and key attribute.
- Five bullet points per product, factual and scannable.
- Description or A+ text drafts, where your account has access.
- Backend search terms, without repeats, brand names you do not own or filler.
- Image set checked and ordered for Amazon's rules.
- Identifier checks: GTIN, EAN, UPC or ASIN, and a GTIN exemption pack where it applies.
- A QA report listing every unresolved attribute.

## How it works

1. **Send your catalogue.** A spreadsheet, store export or supplier file, plus images, and the Amazon markets you sell in.
2. **Fixed quote.** We count products, product types and markets, then send a price before work starts.
3. **We build the files.** The AI engine does the volume; a person reviews every row, especially categories and variations.
4. **You approve.** You review a sample and the QA report. We can upload for you through a user permission, or hand you validated files.
5. **Fix and finish.** If Amazon returns errors, we correct the file and resubmit within the agreed scope.

## What makes Amazon listing different

### Browse nodes and product types

Every product sits in a browse node and a product type. Pick the wrong one and you get the wrong required fields, the wrong fees and sometimes the wrong restrictions. Browse node IDs differ between Amazon markets, so we never copy one from a US file to a UK or Australian one without checking it.

### Parent and child variations

A parent is a container with no price or stock of its own; each child is a sellable SKU. The variation theme, for example Size, Color or Size-Color, must match the attributes on every child. Common mistakes we fix:

| Mistake | Result | Our fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Child missing the theme attribute | Variation rejected | Fill or remove the child |
| Colour typed as free text | Filter mismatch | Map to the category's accepted values |
| Two parents for one product | Split reviews and clutter | Merge into one family |
| Parent given its own price | Error or ignored data | Move price to children |

### Bullets, titles and search terms

Bullets should say what the product is, what it is made of, who it fits and what is in the box, in plain words. Titles must follow category rules, which vary and change over time, so we check the current rules in Seller Central help for your market. Backend search terms are a hidden field for relevant words that do not appear elsewhere; repeating your title there wastes the field, and Amazon enforces a length limit that differs by market.

### Image rules

The main image generally needs a pure white background, the product filling most of the frame, no text or logos added, and enough resolution for zoom (Amazon recommends at least 1,000 pixels on the longest side). Lifestyle and detail shots belong in the secondary slots. See our guide on [product image requirements](/guides/product-image-requirements). We can also produce compliant image sets through [AI product photos](/ai-product-photos).

### GTIN and exemptions

Most listings need a GTIN. If you have none, Amazon has an exemption process in Seller Central for eligible products. Approval is Amazon's decision. For private-label barcodes, GS1 is the standard source. See our [GTIN, MPN and brand](/guides/gtin-mpn-brand) guide.

### Brand Registry and A+ content

A+ content is available to sellers enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry for the brand in question, and eligibility is set per marketplace. If you are enrolled, we draft the module text and image briefs, and you build or approve them in the A+ manager. If you are not enrolled, we prepare the brand and trademark details so you can apply, though Amazon decides.

### Existing ASINs and listing fixes

Before creating anything new, we check whether the product already has an ASIN, because a duplicate page is worse than none. Where it does, we match your offer to it and, if you own the brand, propose content corrections. We can also fix the data behind a suppressed or stranded listing, such as a missing attribute or an invalid identifier, and resubmit. We do not handle account suspension appeals.

### FBA, FBM and what stays with you

Listing data is the same whether you fulfil yourself or use FBA. We do not prepare FBA shipments, labels or inbound plans, and we do not set pricing strategy or run advertising. You open the Seller Central account and complete its identity checks, and we start from there.
