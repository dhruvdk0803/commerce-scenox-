## What you get

- Products from the Wix CSV export turned into Shopify import files: titles, descriptions, SKUs, prices, sale prices, weight, inventory and options.
- Product images, re-hosted on Shopify, with alt text where Wix holds it.
- Collections rebuilt from Wix categories.
- Customer contacts and, if you want them, order history.
- Blog posts and key pages, with the redirect file.
- A QA report of counts and spot checks.

## How it works

1. In the Wix dashboard, open your store products and export to CSV, then send it to us. We tell you if you also need a customers export.
2. We reply with a field map and fixed quote.
3. The AI engine converts the Wix columns to Shopify's format; a person reviews every output.
4. We import to an unpublished Shopify store through a staff account for your review.
5. After approval we load redirects, you switch DNS, and we test.

## What is different about Wix

Wix is a website builder with a store added, which shows in the data.

| Wix | Shopify | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 6 options per product | 3 options per product | Stores using 4 to 6 need a merge, split or metafield |
| Option choices with price surcharges | Variant pricing | Surcharges turned into explicit variant prices |
| Custom text fields on product | Not native | Line-item property or app |
| Product ribbons, badges | Not native | Tags and theme styling, a designer task |
| "Additional info" sections | Metafields or body HTML | Mapped per section title |
| Collections | Collections | One to one |
| Digital products | Digital download app | Files must be re-uploaded; the export does not cover them |
| Wix SEO settings | SEO title, description | Carried over |

Because Wix is often a first store, product data can be thin: short descriptions, inconsistent options, images with filenames like IMG_2041. We can clean that as part of the move through the [catalog cleanup service](/catalog-cleanup), quoted separately.

## URLs and redirects

Wix product pages default to /product-page/product-slug, and blog posts typically to /post/post-slug. Wix lets you customise the product and blog URL patterns in SEO settings, so we check which one your site really uses. Shopify uses /products/, /collections/ and /blogs/news/.

```
Redirect from,Redirect to
/product-page/linen-throw-grey,/products/linen-throw-grey
/category/throws,/collections/throws
/post/spring-styling-tips,/blogs/news/spring-styling-tips
```

Wix addresses usually have no trailing slash and a single path segment after the prefix, so the rules are simple but numerous. Category page paths vary by site, so we confirm them from a crawl. Read more in the [redirect guide](/guides/migration-301-redirects).

## SEO protection

- Crawl the live Wix site before launch and list ranking URLs from Search Console.
- Keep product slugs as handles.
- Copy titles and meta descriptions.
- Check the Shopify sitemap and resubmit it.
- Keep the redirects in place for as long as the old URLs get traffic, which is a year at minimum.

## What we do not do

We do not design themes or write custom apps. Wix Velo code and Wix-only apps have no counterpart on Shopify, so we flag them and recommend a developer or a Shopify app.

## What does not transfer

Wix handles more than products, and some of it has no direct Shopify route. Your design and template do not move. Wix Bookings, Members Area, forms, pricing plans and Velo code have no one-to-one equivalent, so each is replaced with a Shopify app or left behind. We list every Wix feature your site uses at the start, so you can decide what to replace before launch rather than after.

## Cutover and staying live

Your Wix store keeps taking orders while we build and test on an unpublished Shopify store. Near launch we agree a window, you pause product and customer changes, and we re-export so recent orders and sign-ups come across. Then redirects load and DNS is repointed. Plan it for a quiet day.

## Timeline factors

Timing turns on catalog size, option complexity, blog length, number of pages, whether orders are moved, and the DNS and domain arrangement you choose.
