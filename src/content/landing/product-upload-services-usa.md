A US seller usually comes to us with one of three things: a supplier spreadsheet with 800 rows and no consistent naming, a store that needs to be listed on Amazon and Walmart as well, or a backlog nobody on the team wants to touch. All three are routine for us.

## What you get

- Products created on Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce or Magento from your files.
- Titles and descriptions in American English, with inches and pounds first.
- Variants, options and SKUs built to the platform's rules.
- UPC or GTIN fields checked for format, duplicates and gaps.
- Product weights in pounds and ounces, so USPS, UPS and FedEx rates work properly.
- Brand, MPN and condition set consistently, which marketplaces and Google care about.
- Images matched to products, with alt text.
- A QA report of everything we could not resolve, sent with the draft.

## How it works

1. **Send files.** CSV, Excel, a supplier PDF, a distributor feed, or your old store's export.
2. **Fixed quote.** We count products and variants and send a USD price before starting.
3. **We build overnight.** The AI engine does the volume work, and a person reviews every output.
4. **Review in the morning.** Everything lands as draft products. You approve or mark changes.
5. **Publish.** We work through a staff or collaborator account you can remove. We never ask for passwords.

## What is different about selling in the US

### Time-zone handover

Our team is in India. For most US sellers that means we are working while you are offline. In practice: you send files by 5 pm Eastern, answer our single list of questions the next morning, and a finished batch is back the morning after. It is not instant, but it removes a working day from the loop on most projects. We do not promise a fixed number of hours; scope decides.

### Tax and pricing display

US stores show prices before tax, and sales tax is added at checkout by your platform. That makes the upload simple: one price per variant, a compare-at price if you run sales, and a tax category where your platform asks for one. We do not calculate tax. Nexus rules, exemptions and filing are for you and your accountant.

### UPC and GTIN

Marketplaces and Google match products by GTIN, which in the US is usually a 12-digit UPC. Common problems we see: leading zeros stripped by Excel, one UPC pasted across every colour, and supplier codes that fail the check digit. We fix the formatting, flag the rest, and for private-label items point you to GS1 US as the source of valid codes. See our guide on [GTIN, MPN and brand](/guides/gtin-mpn-brand).
