## What jewellery catalogs need

### Variant control

Jewellery is where variants explode. A ring in 3 metals, 2 stone options and 14 sizes is 84 rows for one design. Shopify's three-option limit forces a choice, and a long size list hurts the buying page anyway. Our usual approach: metal and size as options, stone type as a separate product when it changes the price materially, and made-to-order sizes handled through a note rather than a variant. We agree the rule with you before building.

### Ring sizes differ by market

| Market | Typical system | Note |
|---|---|---|
| US | Numeric, such as 6, 7, 8 | Half sizes common |
| UK and Australia | Letters, such as M, N, O | Letter scale, with half sizes |
| India | Its own numeric scale | Does not match the US number, so confirm with your chart |

We keep your selling market's size as the visible label and store the others as secondary data, using a chart you approve.

### Metal and purity

Karat grades such as 14K, 18K and 22K and fineness numbers such as 585, 750 and 916 describe gold purity. We store them as separate filterable fields, not inside the title only, so a shopper can filter by 18K.

### Hallmarking in India

Gold jewellery sold in India falls under BIS hallmarking. The hallmark carries the BIS logo, a purity grade and a six-character alphanumeric HUID. We place the purity grade and any HUID or hallmark text you give us in a consistent field. Coverage and dates have changed in stages, so confirm what applies to your pieces on the BIS website.
