A D2C brand's catalog is never finished. A drop goes out on Friday, last season's items need archiving, the hero product needs better photos, and someone has to tell Google and Meta about all of it. Doing this with an in-house generalist works until launch week, when everything lands at once.

## What you get

- Launch and drop uploads: products, variants, options, prices, collections, tags and metafields, built from your line sheet or spreadsheet.
- Product page quality: consistent titles, benefit-led descriptions, size and care details, alt text and SEO fields.
- Imagery support: white-background mains, lifestyle scenes and consistent crops from your existing photography.
- Feed management for Google Merchant Center and Meta catalogs, so new products appear without manual exports.
- Retention setup as you grow: abandoned cart and post-purchase email, WhatsApp flows where they suit your market.
- A short monthly note listing what was built, what was queried and what is next.

## How it works

1. You send your product sheet, brand notes and the launch calendar for the next quarter.
2. We inspect the files and send a fixed quote, or recommend a monthly plan if the volume is regular.
3. You add a staff or collaborator account for us. No passwords change hands.
4. Our AI engine builds the volume; a person reviews every output against your brand notes.
5. Products land as drafts. You approve, we publish on your timing, and the next drop goes into the queue.

## What D2C catalogs need that others do not

Marketplaces reward uniformity; a D2C store has to feel like a brand. That changes the work.

| D2C reality | What we do about it |
| --- | --- |
| Drops tied to a date | Build as drafts, schedule publication with you, no half-live collections |
| Colour and size variety | Variants and options within platform limits; extra attributes in metafields |
| Bundles and kits | Bundle products with clear component lists rather than vague titles |
| Seasonal archiving | Retire or redirect old products so collection pages stay clean |
| Brand voice | Templates written from your own pages, not generic copy |
| Fast-growing channel mix | One clean catalog that feeds ads, email and WhatsApp |

Brands often reach us after launching twice on messy data. The second drop is where inconsistent tags and missing alt text start costing real time, because filters, collections and ad feeds all depend on the same fields.
