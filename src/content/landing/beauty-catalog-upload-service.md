## What beauty catalogs need

### Shade explosions

A foundation in 30 shades and 2 sizes is 60 variants for one product. Because Shopify allows three options, shade plus size works but adding a finish breaks it, so finish usually becomes a separate product. We name shades the way customers search, adding undertone and depth where you have that data, because "Warm Beige 220" helps more than "Shade 7".

### Ingredients

Ingredient lists follow INCI, the International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients, which gives the names used on labels. We keep the list exactly as the label has it. Cleaning or reordering it is a risk, since it could make the page disagree with the pack. Where a brand supplies "key ingredients" for marketing, we show that separately from the full list.

### Size and pack

Volume units vary: ml, fl oz, g. We store a numeric value and a unit separately. Travel sizes, refills and sets are linked to the full-size product but listed separately, since they have their own price and often their own barcode.

### Claims

Cosmetic claims are regulated, and the rules differ by country. We stick to what you provide and keep wording factual: "formulated with" and "suitable for dry skin" rather than disease or treatment language. Anything that sounds like a medical or guaranteed result is flagged for your decision. This is not legal advice.

### Label information

Packaging declarations such as batch details, manufacturer information and shelf life vary by market. We include fields for what you supply, such as period after opening, so nothing important sits only in an image.

### Marketplaces

Beauty is often a gated or restricted area on marketplaces, with brand approval or documentation required for some categories. Check the channel's current rules before listing. Our [product image guide](/guides/product-image-requirements) covers image rules.

## Skin type, claims and batch details

Shoppers filter beauty by skin type, hair type, concern and finish, so these become structured attributes instead of free text. We also add size and unit, how to use, key ingredients, free-from statements you can support, and whether the product is vegan or cruelty-free only where you hold the evidence.

Expiry, period-after-opening and batch details matter for stores selling to regulated markets. We keep the supplied wording for legal and safety text, such as warnings and mandatory labelling, and avoid rewriting regulated statements. If you sell to the EU, UK, US or India, tell us the markets so we match the format your team uses; we are a catalog service, not a regulatory adviser, so your compliance owner signs off the final wording.
