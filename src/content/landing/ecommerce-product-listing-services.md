Listing on one channel is a project. Listing on five is a data problem. Every new marketplace asks for the same product in a slightly different shape, and the team that keeps all those versions accurate is usually one person with a lot of spreadsheets.

Scenox Commerce takes product listing as a managed service across your own store and the marketplaces you sell on. The aim is one catalog you control, with a correct version on every channel.

## What you get

- A cleaned master catalog that every channel version is built from
- Store listings with titles, descriptions, variants, images and SEO fields
- Marketplace listings in each channel's own template or flat file
- Category and attribute mapping per channel
- Image checks against each marketplace's requirements
- A QA report covering every product, every channel and every flag

## How it works

1. You tell us the channels and send your catalog, in any state. We never ask for passwords.
2. We inspect the data and send a fixed quote that lists channels, product counts and a written delivery date.
3. We build the master catalog. The AI engine does the volume work, a person reviews every output.
4. We generate each channel's version and you approve them in a draft state, an upload template or a staging file.
5. We publish through the access you grant and send the QA report. Marketplace approval then follows that marketplace's own timing.

## One catalog, many channels

The trick to multi-channel listing is to separate what is true about the product from how a channel wants it described.

**Master fields** hold facts: brand, model, materials, dimensions, weight, colour, GTIN where one exists, country of origin, care instructions. These do not change by channel.

**Channel fields** hold presentation: a 200-character title on one marketplace and a 70-character title on another, bullet points where a platform uses them, a category node from each site's own tree, the right image order and ratio.

When a supplier changes a weight or you rename a colour, you change the master once. The channel versions regenerate, rather than being edited in five places.

| Element | Own store | Marketplace |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Brand voice, SEO-led | Channel pattern, often keyword-first |
| Category | Your collections | The marketplace's category tree |
| Attributes | Whatever you filter on | Channel-required attributes, sometimes dozens |
| Images | Your style guide | Channel rules, often white background first |
| Identifiers | Optional | Often mandatory: GTIN, brand, model |

## Mistakes we see on multi-channel catalogs

Most problems are boring. Titles stuffed for one channel and pasted onto another. Variants that exist in the store but are flattened into separate listings elsewhere. Missing GTINs in required fields. Category choices made by guesswork, which then limit where a product can appear. Images that are fine on the store and fail a marketplace's background rule.
